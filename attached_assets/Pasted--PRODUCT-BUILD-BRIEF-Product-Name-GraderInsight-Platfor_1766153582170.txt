WHAT THIS APP DOES

GraderInsight is a web-based application designed to streamline the grading process for writing assignments. It allows educators to efficiently upload assignments in bulk, anonymize student submissions for unbiased grading, and grade against customizable rubrics. The platform will automatically generate quick summaries of writing quality and provide actionable insights, significantly reducing grading time while enhancing the quality and consistency of feedback provided to students.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THE PROBLEM
Professors, instructors, and teachers face significant time constraints when grading a high volume of writing assignments, often spending hours providing feedback that, while valuable, is time-consuming to generate. This inefficiency not only reduces the time available for other teaching responsibilities but also impacts the depth and consistency of feedback provided, leading to a less refined grading insight. GraderInsight aims to dramatically reduce grading time for 100 assignments across 5 courses from 2 hours to 45 minutes, thereby improving grading efficiency and feedback quality.

TARGET USER
Our primary target users are Professors, Instructors, and Teachers at universities, colleges, and high schools. These individuals are typically aged 30-65, tech-literate but time-poor, and often responsible for teaching multiple courses with large class sizes. Their main pain points include the overwhelming time commitment for grading written assignments, the challenge of providing consistent and detailed feedback to all students, and the desire to allocate more time to teaching, curriculum development, and student interaction rather than administrative grading tasks.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CORE FEATURES
1. Bulk Assignment Upload - Allows educators to upload multiple student assignments simultaneously, supporting common document formats like PDF, DOCX, and TXT, and organizing them by course and assignment.

2. Student Anonymization - Automatically redacts or replaces student names on submitted assignments to promote unbiased grading and reduce implicit bias.

3. Customizable Rubric Management - Enables users to create, import, or modify grading rubrics, associating them with specific assignments for consistent and structured evaluation.

4. Efficient Rubric-Based Grading Interface - Provides an intuitive interface for applying rubric criteria to assignments, allowing for quick selection of scores and predefined feedback comments, and the ability to add custom notes.

5. Automated Writing Summary Generation - Utilizes natural language processing (NLP) to generate a concise summary of the writing's strengths and weaknesses based on predefined rubric criteria and common writing issues.

6. Grading Progress and Analytics Dashboard - Offers an overview of grading progress for all courses and assignments, including time spent grading, average scores, and common feedback trends.

7. Export Graded Assignments and Feedback - Allows educators to export graded assignments, scores, and detailed feedback in various formats for easy integration with Learning Management Systems (LMS) or record-keeping.

ADMIN PANEL (/admin) - For Administrators Only

Purpose: Manage users and system settings

Features:
- User management table (view all users with email, name, status)
- Search and filter users by email, name, role, or status
- User actions: view details, suspend/activate, delete account
- Role management: promote to admin, demote to user
- Bulk user creation via CSV upload
- Password reset and change password for users
- Admin-only route protection (redirect non-admins)

Security:
- Admin role verification on all admin routes
- Prevent self-demotion and removal of last admin
- Secure edge functions with admin role checks
