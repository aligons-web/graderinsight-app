2-Year Technical College – Research Paper Rubric
Total: 100 Points | Minimum Length: 300 Words
Policy Notice: Assignments that do not meet the minimum word count may not earn full credit in Criterion 4.

1. Writing Quality & Clarity — 25 Points
Evaluates correctness, clarity, and professional tone
Sentence structure and clarity
Grammar and subject-verb agreement
Conciseness (avoids unnecessary wordiness)
Word choice appropriate for an academic audience

Scoring Guide
23–25: Clear, polished, and professional
18–22: Minor errors that do not affect meaning
13–17: Frequent errors affecting readability
0–12: Writing significantly interferes with comprehension

2. Organization & Development — 25 Points
Evaluates logical structure and idea progression
Clear thesis or purpose statement
Strong topic sentences
Supporting details are relevant and sufficient
Logical transitions between ideas

Scoring Guide
23–25: Well-organized and fully developed
18–22: Generally clear with minor gaps
13–17: Weak structure or limited development
0–12: Disorganized or incomplete

3. Source Use & Documentation — 25 Points
Evaluates research integration and citation accuracy
Credible and relevant sources used
Accurate in-text citations
Properly formatted references/works cited page
Evidence is explained and connected to ideas

Scoring Guide
23–25: Sources integrated smoothly and correctly
18–22: Minor citation or integration issues
13–17: Weak or inconsistent source use
0–12: Missing, incorrect, or improperly used sources

4. Technical Accuracy & Assignment Requirements — 25 Points
Ensures originality, compliance, and completeness
Minimum word count met (300+ words)
Assignment instructions followed
Plagiarism check passed
AI-use compliance (if applicable)
Clear summary or conclusion

Scoring Guide
23–25: All requirements fully met
18–22: One minor requirement issue
13–17: Multiple requirement issues
0–12: Major requirements unmet

Late Submission Policy (Research Paper)
Up to 24 hours late: −10 points
25–72 hours late: −20 points
More than 72 hours late: Not accepted unless prior approval is granted

Revision Policy (Research Paper)
One revision allowed if original submission scored below 85 points
Maximum revision score: 85 points
Revised submission must be turned in within 7 days of feedback
Original feedback must be addressed for revision credit

Clear main idea or purpose
Logical paragraph structure
Topic sentences
Smooth transitions

Scoring Guide

0–12: Disorganized or unclear

Relevant examples or reasoning
Evidence of critical thinking or reflection
Audience awareness

Scoring Guide

13–17: Limited development
0–12: Underdeveloped or off-topic

13–17: Multiple issues
0–12: Major requirements unmet

HIGH SCHOOL PRESENTATION RUBRIC
Total: 100 Points | Time Target: 3–5 Minutes

1. Content Design & Visual Aids — 25 Points
Text is readable (generally 24-pt or larger)
Slides are not overcrowded with text
Use of images, charts, or visuals
Slides relate clearly to the topic
Presenter does not read slides word-for-word
Scoring
23–25: Clear, neat, and effective visuals
18–22: Mostly clear with minor issues
13–17: Overcrowded or inconsistent
0–12: Visuals distract or confuse

2. Delivery & Engagement — 30 Points
Voice is clear and understandable
Appropriate speaking pace
Some eye contact with audience
Limited filler words
Demonstrates preparation and effort
Video Presentations (if used):
Speaker visible and audible
Minimal background distractions
Scoring
27–30: Confident and engaging
21–26: Clear with some nervousness
15–20: Limited engagement
0–14: Difficult to follow

3. Time Management — 15 Points
Presentation fits within time range (±1 minute allowed)
Content is balanced
Clear beginning and ending

4. Content Quality & Flow — 30 Points
Clear main idea or purpose
Logical order of ideas
Accurate and relevant information
Clear conclusion

Late & Revision Policy (High School)
1 day late: −10 points
2+ days late: teacher discretion
One revision allowed (max score: 85)
